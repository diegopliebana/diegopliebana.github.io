Profile

I am a Distinguished Researcher at Universidad Carlos III de Madrid (UC3M). My research focuses on Artificial Intelligence for games, with particular interests in Statistical Forward Planning, Monte Carlo Tree Search (MCTS), Rolling Horizon Evolutionary Algorithms, Reinforcement Learning, General Game Playing, and Artificial Intelligence for modern tabletop games.

My work investigates decision making in complex environments and how forward models, search, abstraction and learning can be used to build more capable and general agents. More recently, this research has expanded towards automated playtesting, quantitative game analysis, AI-assisted game design and Large Language Models.

I have published more than 150 research papers across Game AI, computational intelligence and related areas. I have also developed and organised international Game AI competitions, created open-source research frameworks, supervised doctoral researchers, and worked extensively with industry.

Research

My research has centred on Artificial Intelligence methods that make decisions by reasoning about possible futures. Major themes include:

  • Monte Carlo Tree Search: long-term planning, heuristic bias, tree reuse, multi-objective search, state and action abstraction, portfolio methods and Elastic MCTS.
  • Evolutionary Planning: Rolling Horizon Evolutionary Algorithms and their application to real-time and general game playing.
  • General Game Playing: agents capable of operating across previously unseen games rather than specialising in a single environment.
  • Strategy Games: abstraction, planning and general decision-making methods for complex turn-based games.
  • Tabletop Games: planning, Multi-Agent Reinforcement Learning, game characterisation, automated playtesting and AI-assisted game design.
  • AI and Game Design: quantitative analysis, balancing, digital twins, quality-diversity methods and Large Language Models.

A survey I co-authored on Monte Carlo Tree Search has become one of the foundational references in the field.

Selected Research Projects

Tabletop Games Framework (TAG) — ongoing

An open-source research ecosystem for Artificial Intelligence in modern tabletop games. TAG supports planning, Reinforcement Learning through PyTAG, quantitative game analysis, automated playtesting and AI-assisted game design.

Stratega

A configurable framework for General Strategy Game Playing developed within research on Abstract Forward Models. Work using Stratega includes Portfolio Search, state and action abstraction, Elastic MCTS and automated point-cost estimation.

Tribes

A research-oriented strategy game inspired by The Battle of Polytopia, used to investigate Statistical Forward Planning, Portfolio MCTS, Progressive Unpruning and MAP-Elites for diverse play-styles.

General Video Game Artificial Intelligence (GVGAI)

A major international benchmark and competition ecosystem for General Video Game Playing, spanning planning, learning without forward models, multiplayer games and Procedural Content Generation.

Physical Travelling Salesman Problem (PTSP)

An early benchmark for real-time planning that supported research on MCTS with macro-actions, multi-objective MCTS and procedurally generated evaluation environments.

Knowledge Transfer and Tabletop R&D

I am a co-founder and director of Tabletop R&D, a Queen Mary University of London spin-out created to transfer research on Artificial Intelligence for tabletop games into practical tools for professional designers and publishers.

The company builds digital twins of tabletop games and uses automated AI playtesting to generate large-scale quantitative evidence about balance, strategies, scoring systems and gameplay dynamics.

This work grew directly from research conducted with the TAG framework and provides a pathway from academic Game AI research to applications in the creative industries.
